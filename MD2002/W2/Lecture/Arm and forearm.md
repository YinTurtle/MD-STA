# 1️⃣ First learn the **nerve territories (compartments rule)**

Muscles in the limb are usually innervated **by compartment**.

### Arm

|Compartment|Action|Nerve|
|---|---|---|
|Anterior arm|Flexion|**Musculocutaneous nerve**|
|Posterior arm|Extension|**Radial nerve**|

So immediately you know:

**Musculocutaneous**

- Biceps brachii
- Brachialis
- Coracobrachialis

**Radial**

- Triceps
- Anconeus

---

# 2️⃣ Then learn the **forearm rule**

Forearm muscles divide into **flexors vs extensors**.

### Posterior forearm

All **extensors → Radial nerve**

Examples:

- Extensor digitorum
- Extensor carpi radialis longus/brevis
- Extensor carpi ulnaris
- Extensor indicis
- Extensor digiti minimi

👉 So if you see **extension**, think **radial nerve**.

---

### Anterior forearm

Mostly **Median nerve**

Examples:

- Flexor carpi radialis
    
- Palmaris longus
    
- Flexor digitorum superficialis
    
- Flexor pollicis longus
    
- Pronator teres
    
- Pronator quadratus
    

👉 If it's a **flexor or pronator**, think **median nerve**.

---

# 3️⃣ Then memorise the **exceptions**

These are the only things you really need to actively memorise.

### Exceptions in anterior forearm

**Ulnar nerve supplies:**

- **Flexor carpi ulnaris**
    
- **Medial half of flexor digitorum profundus**
    

Mnemonic:

**"FCU and FDP to the Ulnar nerve"**

---

# 4️⃣ Remember the important pattern

The **radial nerve dominates extension**.

So:

|Movement|Nerve|
|---|---|
|Extension|Radial|
|Flexion/pronation|Median|
|Medial flexors|Ulnar|

---

# 5️⃣ Simple memory framework

### ARM

**BBC**

Biceps  
Brachialis  
Coracobrachialis

→ **Musculocutaneous**

Everything else posterior → **Radial**

---

### FOREARM

Posterior → **Radial**

Anterior → **Median**

Except:

**FCU + FDP (medial half)** → **Ulnar**

## Cubital Fossa
- **Superior:** line between medial and lateral epicondyles
    
- **Lateral:** **brachioradialis**
    
- **Medial:** **pronator teres**
    
- **Floor:** **brachialis** and **supinator**
    
- **Roof:** fascia, skin, superficial veins

---

## Carpal Tunnel
### **Nerve**
- **Median nerve**
### **Tendons (9)**
- **4 × flexor digitorum superficialis (FDS)**
    
- **4 × flexor digitorum profundus (FDP)**
    
- **1 × flexor pollicis longus (FPL)**
