A researcher is performing a genetic analysis on the gene the codes for the RAS protein. He notices a certain mutation that affects the RAS-signalling cascade in a kinase-linked receptor leading to an increase in tumorigenesis. What is the entire pathway that causes this change?
Transforming Growth Factor Alpha (TGFa) --> Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor (EGFR) --> RAS --> RAF --> MEK --> ERK
